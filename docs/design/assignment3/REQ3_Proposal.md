We propose the idea of crafting items using other items as an Action for Player using a Crafting Table. 
The Crafting Table can be used to craft items on hand and when it's dropped.
The Crafting Recipe must be predetermined during the setup before the game runs.

To craft a super bow, The Player first needs to obtain
3 Stick items and 3 StringItem items which then outputs a Craft Action option to
craft the super bow. The Super Bow has a minimum range of 2 tiles and maximum range of 10 tiles, and deals 20 damage
but has the same 25 hit rate as a normal bow. 

To craft a Torch, the Player needs to obtain a stick item which
then outputs a Craft Action option to craft the Torch. The Player can craft the torch on-hand only if the player
is on fire, otherwise it can craft when the Crafting Table is dropped on the ground.

To craft a Bedroll, the Player needs 5 Stick items to craft a Bedroll. However, it can only craft the bedroll
if both the hydration level and warmth level is at most 5.
