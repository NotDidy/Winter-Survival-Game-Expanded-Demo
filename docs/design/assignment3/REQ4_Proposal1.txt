We try to incorporate a necromancy mechanic where when use weapons that are cursed(could be predetermined before or crafted)
and kill a creature, there would be a chance the creature would resurrect into a ghost and ally with the player.

For example, A Player finds a Cursed Sword somewhere within the game world and decides to use it against
other Creatures and manages to kill it. The creature would then resurrect and fight for the player and have its own unique
set of attributes (Frost Coated Attacks). The odds of a creature resurrecting can also be implemented to where it depends
on what the max hitpoints the creature has, the more hitpoints the less likely.

Technical implementation (not finalized)
The way it would be implemented would be similar to how we inflict statuses with weapons. If the creature dies due to
the AttackAction from the Player using the Cursed Sword, the creature will undergo a ResurrectAction and depending on how
much the max HP on the creature, the creature will resurrect in the same position within the GameMap. There will be a concrete
Ghost class that has its own special traits (maybe other behaviours) like frost attacks that also heal up to a certain
value upon hit
