We also would like to incorporate a Cursed Weapon mechanic where if the Player has the Cursed Weapon within its inventory
for more than a certain amount of rounds, the item drops and a ghost spawns near the player and attacks the player.

For example, the Cursed Weapon would have a really high hit chance and damage value but if it is not dropped
within 5 rounds from when it got picked up by the player, the item would drop and the spawn a ghost within an adjacent
block of the player. The Player would have to kill the ghost first and only then would the weapon drop again to be used
but the mechanic still applies.

Technical Implementation (Draft)
There would be a Cursed Weapon concrete class (Cursed Sword for now) that operates exactly like a normal weapon. Picking up
the weapon would automatically inflict a Cursed status onto the weapon and would start a counter. The counter would tick as the game progresses
until the tick reaches 0 in which the weapon spawns a ghost around the player and destroy itself from the inventory. Upon
killing the ghost, the ghost will drop the Cursed Weapon on the same position it got killed.
